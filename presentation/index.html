ORION MIS • PROJECT OVERVIEW SLIDE 02 / 18
02 / Problem Statement

From Fragmented Effort to a Connected Campus

Traditional legacy university operations introduce operational friction, delay, and security vulnerabilities.

TRADITIONAL SYSTEM

  • ❌ Paper-based operations & physical record keeping
  • ❌ Manual paper attendance registers
  • ❌ Slow, error-prone manual report generation
  • ❌ Isolated, poor inter-department communication
  • ❌ Lack of centralized data management
  • ❌ High security and compliance risks

ORION MIS SOLUTION

  • ⚡ Automated digital cloud workflows
  • ⚡ Real-time biometric & digital attendance
  • ⚡ Instant PDF & Excel report generation
  • ⚡ Unified real-time communication channels
  • ⚡ Centralized command & data intelligence
  • ⚡ Enterprise security with JWT & RBAC
